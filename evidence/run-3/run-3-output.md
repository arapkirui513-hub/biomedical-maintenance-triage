- **Repeated equipment IDs:** EQ-002 occurs in MR-002 and MR-013; MR-013 is treated as a possible continuation of the prior unresolved occlusion/delivery fault. EQ-007 occurs in MR-007 and MR-014; MR-014 is considered alongside the prior unresolved battery indication. - **Ambiguous/incomplete reports:** MR-004 does not confirm that manual spot checks are an effective alternative; MR-005 lacks medication identity and interruption duration; MR-013 lacks current symptom, status, action, clinical context, and impact; MR-014 relies on variable recollection with no measured charging data and no statement that MR-007 was resolved. MR-006 does not localize the issue to accessory versus device. MR-009 lacks independent output measurement.
- **Register/data-quality conflicts:** All 14 IDs and equipment types match register entries. Department, manufacturer, model, criticality, assigned team, maintenance frequency, last-maintenance date, and operational-status fields are blank. MR-009 claims EQ-009 criticality is medium, but the current register field is blank; this is an unverified report claim, not verified register data.
- **Uncertainty:** Lowest confidence is MR-013 (0.43), followed by MR-005 (0.64), MR-004 (0.68), and MR-014 (0.69). Missing facts and their effect on classification are stated in the table.
- **Run 3 routing outcomes:** MR-010, MR-003, and MR-011 are routed to Biomedical Engineering because they are registered assets. This resolves the portable-device/external-damage boundary for MR-010 and makes Biomedical Engineering the primary asset owner for the registered analyzers; vendor service may remain a technical escalation. MR-004 is High pending confirmation that spot checks are an effective alternative; stable status alone does not establish that condition.
