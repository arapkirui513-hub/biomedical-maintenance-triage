## Review Notes

- **Repeated equipment IDs considered:** EQ-002 appears in MR-002 and MR-013. MR-013 is treated as a possible continuation/recurrence of MR-002's unresolved occlusion and delivery-interruption fault. EQ-007 appears in MR-007 and MR-014. MR-014's reported charging delay is considered alongside MR-007's unresolved battery fault. EQ-004 and EQ-012 have similar SpO2 dropout descriptions but are different registered units; their cases are assessed independently.
- **Important cross-source conflicts:** The EQ-009 report's additional notes say the register lists criticality as medium, but the supplied register's criticality cell for EQ-009 is blank. The stated medium value therefore could not be independently verified. Following policy, the reported current clinical context (a fully oxygen-dependent ICU patient) takes precedence over any static criticality value; this conflict is flagged for inventory-owner verification. The register also has blank department, manufacturer, model, criticality, assigned-team, maintenance-frequency, last-maintenance-date, and operational-status fields for all listed equipment. Reported types and IDs match the register; report-department values are not independently corroborated by the register.
- **Cases with insufficient information:** MR-005 lacks medication identity, interruption duration, and evidence linking the pause to battery/power. MR-013 provides no current symptom, status, action, or clinical context. MR-014 provides recollection rather than measured charge-time data and does not state whether the prior battery fault was resolved. MR-004 and MR-006 do not localize the fault to accessory versus device. MR-009 has no independent output measurement. These gaps are itemized in the table and confidence reduced accordingly.
- **Particularly high uncertainty:** MR-013 (0.43) has only a nonspecific request and depends heavily on the unresolved MR-002 history. MR-005 (0.64) has a documented interruption but lacks the medication and duration needed to determine its consequence. MR-009 (0.71) has an important but unverified low-output observation and a register criticality field conflict. MR-014 (0.69) is based on variable staff recollection with passing self-tests and unresolved prior battery-fault status.

The register supplied no assigned-team values, so teams above are selected from the policy's fault-domain routing rules rather than copied from the register. Reported observations are kept distinct from confirmed technical faults throughout.
